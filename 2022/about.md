# About

The first edition of the workshop on Bridges and Gaps between Formal and Computational Linguistics (BriGap-1) was held in conjunction with [ESSLLI 2022](https://2022.esslli.eu/), in Galway, Ireland.

The workshop was part of the GdR LIFT activities on formal and computational linguistics, alongside the monthly online [ILFC seminar](https://ilfc.github.io/), also organized by Timothée Bernard and Grégoire Winterstein.

The organisation of this workshop was made possible thanks to funding from [GdR LIFT](https://gdr-lift.loria.fr/), the [Laboratoire de linguistique formelle](http://www.llf.cnrs.fr/) and [Labex EFL](https://www.labex-efl.fr/).
